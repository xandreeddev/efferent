# @xandreed/plugin-sessions

Core's `Sessions` port over the host's `SessionLog`: one event log per session, one open turn at a time (`BeginTurn`'s direct User, Queued user input, Inbox notices or Task, told apart by `Match.valueTags` in `askedOf`), turns owned by a lease or by the process that began them, the inbox, forks, resumes, `TurnActivity` (the turns this instance holds open or is beginning) and `TurnShutdown` (how it stops), plus owner-observed `WaitingInput`. The host admits every turn through `TurnAdmission`. Node 24 and Bun; layer `plugin-sessions` in `foundry.config.ts` (imports core, with declared `node:os` and `node:process` in the process-liveness adapter).

## Rules

- Change the head's `sessions` state only in a guarded commit (`guarded`: compare-and-swap on the revision, planned again after a conflict) with the events that justify it; no backend reads that state.
- Creation atomically stamps core's `STORED_FORMAT` in `SessionsState`. Every owned operation accepts only this format (`isUnsupportedFormat`, `session.version`), including reads. Other formats are refused without mutation or migration; hosts configure fresh stores. A state-shape change bumps `STORED_FORMAT` (skill `change-event`).

- Fail with `SessionLogError` only for the store (the log's own, or stored data that does not decode: `session.state`, `session.decode`, its `cause` kept); `guarded` gives up as `SessionContended` after `retries` conflicts; a value of the plugin's own that does not encode, or an answer the log's contract rules out, is a defect (`Effect.orDie`, `Effect.die`).
- Read a stored record's fields through core's `readStored` (`turn.ended`, `inbox.queued`), and look a turn up by its key or run through `readStoredKeys`, which decodes the keys alone, never the message and its images; never read `event.data.<field>` unchecked. A row that does not decode fails with `session.decode`.
- Pass a known snapshot or fork ceiling as `SessionReadQuery.through` before reading event data. Filtering after a read is too late for a decorating store that hydrates blobs. Keep the final sequence filter as defense for custom backends, and never compare sequence numbers across sessions.
- Turn lookups that decode keys alone (`startsOf`, `turnsOf`: keys, runs, inputs, origins, resumed turns) read with `SessionReadQuery.media: "references"`, so a media-storing log reads no image for a begin; the one `turn.started` a duplicate-key check compares is read again whole (`startedWhole`). Never decode a message or its images from a key-only read. `src/sessions.media-store.test.ts` counts the blob reads of a begin and covers a lost blob.
- `SessionsState.memorySnapshot`, when present, identifies a `memory.snapshot` publication in that head's own session. Every state transition preserves it. The writer derives it from the final stamped draft offset inside each CAS attempt, before omitting unchanged state: the snapshot and pointer commit together, including after a rebase. A snapshot allocates no memory entry id.
- `TurnWriter.memorySnapshot` reads a pointer before its own `turn.started`, or an ancestor pointer within that ancestor's fork ceiling. Read only the exact pointed row with `after`, `through`, kind and limit; an ineligible future pointer returns None before fetching its payload or media. A selected wrong-session pointer, missing/wrong row or invalid snapshot data fails with `SessionLogError` `session.decode`, its cause retained. An own overall head rejected during begin retains the existing `session.state` behavior. The SDK validates actual anchor/frontier memory rows and retained contents; this adapter validates their authorized lineage order. Existing full `history` and fresh durable `snapshot` reads retain their meanings.
- Name the session's incarnation in every commit: `guarded` the head it planned on, the writer the one its turn began in (`TurnWriterInput.incarnation`), and a rebase that finds another closes the writer as `removed`; a session removed and created again under its id never takes a stale writer's events.
- Write a turn's events only through its writer (`makeTurnWriter`): one fiber in queue order, consecutive appends in one commit, the framework's reserved kinds refused unless marked (`ReservedKind`). The closing drafts a turn holds (`closing`, its reply) are stored only by the end's own commit, right before `turn.ended`: never by a flush, kept when an end is refused by the store, dropped by a close from elsewhere.
- Keep the writer's state in its one phase (`WriterPhase`: open, ending, failed, closed, ended; open and ending carry the closing drafts): move it only by the pure transitions of `src/turn-writer.entity.functions.ts` under `Ref.modify`, then run what the transition planned (queue an item, commit the end, settle the callers). Never a `SynchronizedRef`: the consumer fiber latches failures while an admitter holds the admission permit, blocked on a full queue; the `admission` semaphore only orders admissions.
- Raise the writer's failures as `TurnError`'s tags, never as a `HarnessError` of their codes: `TurnClosed` with its reason (`ended` for its own end or a closed scope), `ReservedKind`, `SessionContended`, the log's own `SessionLogError`; a `HarnessError` only for the writer's own state (`session.encode`, `session.state`) and a write that died (`session.defect`), with its `cause`. Match them with `Effect.catchTag`, never by code.
- Judge an open turn by its own record (a lease end, or none and its holder process) through `isHeld`, never by the reading instance's ownership; a begin with `takeOver` (the host's word that the holder is gone) closes a turn still held in its opening commit (`reaped`), with `turn.takeover` and no attempt counted, and closes this instance's own writer of it at once. A named takeover (`{ turn, runId }`) applies only while that exact turn is the open one; any other held turn makes the begin `SessionBusy`. A drain passes its `takeOver` to its first turn only.
- Refuse a busy begin, never replace the open turn: `SessionBusy`, `TurnDuplicate`, `KeyConflict`, `RunIdReused`, `NotResumable`, `NothingPending`. Save user work explicitly through `enqueue`; it does not begin a turn.
- A keyed `Sessions.transact` stores its key in the commit of its decision: `transact.keyed` (key, command, how many events follow) first, then the decision's drafts, so a key is never stored without them nor them without it. Look the key up inside the guarded plan, before `decide`: a repeat calls no `decide` and writes nothing (`TransactDuplicate` with the events read back after the record, `TransactConflict` for another canonical command), and a racing repeat finds the record after its commit conflicts. Keep transact keys in their own record kind, apart from `turn.started` keys, and `Decided.events` without the record. The keyed refusals appear only in the type of a call given a key (`TransactRefusal`, through the overloaded `transact`).
- Normalize queued messages before storage. One input id names its normalized message, delivery and source forever: identical enqueue returns false even after completion; another payload or an id used by a notice is `InputConflict`. Keep the payload only in `inbox.queued.input` (legacy `content` empty), with its delivery marker in the head; never duplicate attachment bytes in the head.
- A Queued begin claims exactly its named input in the opening CAS, with origin user, its exact message and no claimed notices. Resolve its actual initial or retry key before admission, then recheck its pending claim in the opening plan. Carry full provenance on `AdmittedTurn.input`, only its id on `turn.started.input` and `TurnToAdmit.input`; include identity in the key fingerprint.
- Reclaim a queued input when an ordinary User begin resumes its root, in the same opening commit. A successful resume acknowledges it once; a failed resume returns it. A manually resumed cancellation keeps provenance without recreating an acknowledged slot.
- Keep `Inbox` begins and `admitted.claimed` notice-only. `drain` selects `all`, `inputs` or `notices`; queued inputs run singly in enqueue order, with an untaken steer still eligible for its own queued user turn. All selects the oldest waiting kind, and an Inbox then takes the notice batch.
- `TurnWriter.steers` reads fresh pending steers in queue order, including other instances' commits, without recording an attempt. Exclude next input, notices and claimed initial input. `take(entries)` refreshes/rebases before eligibility checks; a stale selection returns false without a commit or attempt.
- A successful take validates one exact user `Message` per distinct input id, with this turn/run and fresh entry ids; one CAS stores every message, its `inbox.taken` receipt and slot removal. Keep messages and queue consumption in one commit, and never call memory or flush the writer from its queued decision. A taken steer is acknowledged by that durable message across failed turns; the receipt's outer turn anchors later `TurnDuplicate`, before run-id reuse checks, without inventing a turn.started input link.
- Count notice attempts by the existing write/interruption rules; drop a notice once `inbox.attempts` turns took it and failed. Untaken queued input is never dropped by attempts: failed, partial and interrupted turns return it; completion, waiting for an answer or explicit cancellation acknowledges the input claimed by that turn. Preserve an early answer and the absent delivery marker of legacy notice slots.
- Keep `TurnActivity` exact: a begin counts from the request (before its first store read, which stays interruptible) until its writer is handed over, then the turn, held by its session's id, owner and incarnation and its number (a session created again under its id numbers from 1 again), until the writer's scope closes (`awaitIdle` relies on it); `TurnShutdown` closes each held turn through the writer of that very session.
- Close an open turn from outside through `closeOpen` (a cancel, a shutdown): one guarded commit that reaps a turn nobody holds instead, else stores its `turn.ended`, then closes this instance's writer of it with the same reason.
- Seal a turn only for a held completed or partial reply (`TurnWriter.seal`, `sealable`): one state-only commit stores the ending and the closing drafts held then (the reply's; never a `memory.snapshot`, whose row needs the holder's own pointer commit) in the head (`HeldTurn.sealed`, `SealedTurn`, stored format 17), and the ending in the writer's open or ending phase (`sealed`), before the turn's work after its reply. From then on every close ends it with that ending, never interrupted or failed: `closeOpen` (a cancel, a shutdown, a time limit) ends a sealed writer of this instance through its own end, the drafts it still holds stored (`endSealedLocal`), else plans the sealed ending with the sealed drafts (`sealedDrafts`); a reap (`turn.lease`, `turn.takeover`) stores the sealed drafts before `turn.ended`, so the reply survives its holder, and the resume check sees the turn finished. A refused begin, whose plan's reap is dropped with it, reaps a sealed turn its holder left in a commit of its own (`reapSealed`), so a refused resume of that finished turn, or of a queued input it already answered, still stores it, and the inbox drainer never retries a resume of it; a begin that opens its turn reaps it in its opening commit as before, with no extra read. The writer's own end, and its scope's, prefer the sealed ending to the caller's. Keep these changes in the writer and the close helpers.
- Run a turn's time limit (the begin's `timeLimitMs`, else `turns.timeLimitMs`) as a fiber in the turn's scope, from when its writer is handed over: past it, close the turn as interrupted with `turn.time-limit`, an attempt counted, its writer's `TurnClosed` `timed-out` (`closeForTimeLimit`); the turn's end (its writer's commit that leaves it closed), a close from elsewhere (`TurnWriter.closed`) or the scope's close stops the timer.
- Remove through `remove`: the log's removal runs interruptibly (`restore`), then, on any outcome, once the session is gone, `letGoLocally` (its let-go turns, waiting input, writers closed as `removed`, waiters) runs uninterruptibly; a later remove with the `SessionRef` that finishes a removal lets go the same way (only that life's writers while the owner's later life holds the id).
- Let go of a turn by its number and run id (`letGo`, `ReleasedTurn`): a turn this instance let go of is held by nobody, never a later turn opened under that number. A writer closed because its session was removed (`OpenWriter.removed`) is not let go of: no commit would ever forget it. A begin whose opening commit failed with `SessionLogError` lets the turn it planned go (a stalled write may still land); a later begin that opens that very turn forgets it (`forgetOpened`).
- Stop for a shutdown in one phase (`stopping`: running, refusing, interrupting): once refusing, refuse every begin before the host's admission and again inside its opening plan (`shutdownRefusal`), never a delivery; once interrupting, close every turn this instance holds, and any begin whose writer is handed over after, as interrupted with `turn.shutdown` and no attempt counted (`closeForShutdown`), its writer closed even when the store refused the end.
- A keep-alive is queued in order with the turn's writes: keep one `write` or `transact` shorter than the lease.
- Signal `changes` through one hub per followed session, keyed by its id and owner, `PubSub.sliding(1)` held in an `RcMap` (released with its last follower), subscribed only after the owner check (`owned`: `SessionMissing` for another owner): a commit wakes only its own session's followers, and a follower that does not pull holds one signal. Never `dropping` (it loses the last signal) nor a `SubscriptionRef` (it signals a follower on subscribing, before any commit).
- Keep the in-memory `SessionChannel` (`SessionChannelMemoryLive`, `makeMemorySessionChannel`) as `changes` keeps its signals: one `PubSub.sliding(capacity)` per followed session in an `RcMap` keyed by the whole address (id and owner), released with its last follower; a publish to a session nobody follows takes no hub (`RcMap.getOption`) and is dropped. Never store, replay or block on a payload: the journal is the only record. Change its behaviour by adding the check to core's `sessionChannelConformance` first.
- Trace each `Sessions` call but `changes` as `sessions.<op>` (`Effect.fn`; the `onSession` pipeable puts `sessionAttributes` on its span and its logs), each writer commit as `turn_writer.commit` under the span of the call that queued it (`underCaller`: the writer's fiber was forked under the begin), and the writer's `flush` and `end` as `turn_writer.flush` and `turn_writer.end`, all with `turnAttributes`; the writer's fibers carry the turn's ids on their spans and logs. A log line names no id: the ids are its annotations. Trace nothing per append or per `write`.
- Keep native process access in `process-liveness.adapter.ts`, with declared `node:os` and `node:process` dependencies. Hosts may inject `ProcessLiveness`.
- Change behaviour by adding the check to core's `sessionsConformance` first, with a careless instance in `src/sessions.conformance.test.ts` that it catches.

- `Sessions.waiting` broadcasts one initial signal after subscribing, then coalesced local commits that change the open turn or an inbox slot (`waitingChanged`), or create or remove a session, through a scoped sliding-one hub. A turn's own appends and lease renewals signal nothing there. Keep per-session `changes` commit-only: it wakes after every commit, a writer's included.
- `WaitingInput` observes only successfully owner-checked addresses (their id and owner, never an incarnation: a session created again under them is still refreshed), refreshes their unclaimed delivery-marked slots, excludes notices and forgets removed sessions. It keeps every session last seen with waiting input (a `HashMap`, never a map copied per read) and only the `inbox.recentSessions` most recently observed quiet ones, so a refresh reads a bounded set. Storage failures stay typed; cached knowledge is infallible and never resets on a failed read. Its idle wait polls foreign commits too (`inbox.pollIntervalMs`). No stored state is added for activity.
- `InboxDrainLive` is an optional host-composed layer requiring Sessions and InboxRunner, with explicit owners, fixed scoped workers, per-session serialization, fair scheduling and bounded backoff. Wakes never reset retry deadlines; startup takeover authority is consumed once and names the open turn the startup sweep saw (`{ turn, runId }`), never one this instance holds (its `TurnActivity`). Preserve input after partial/failed/interrupted attempts and intentionally cancelled resume branches, but park it (`isParked`: still queued, skipped so later input runs) after `attempts` failed or partial runs or a deliberate stop. Resume a root only when the latest user turn's holder was gone (`turn.lease`, `turn.takeover`, `turn.shutdown`, open with no live holder), it was interrupted at or before the startup sweep's sequence number for that session, or it is a failed resume within `attempts`: never after a time limit or an in-process interruption. A runner that interrupts itself is marked tried (an empty `transact`) before the end. Scans reuse a session's last target while its sequence number and open turn are unchanged.

## Public surface

- `InboxDrainLive(options)`, `InboxDrainConfig`, `InboxDrainOptions`, `inboxDrainDefaults`; no owner or takeover authority is inferred.
- `sessionsPlugin` (also the default export) and `SessionsPluginLive` (its `.live` layer); `SessionsLive(config, { liveness })` for hosts that compose layers; `processLiveness` (type `ProcessLiveness`).
- Core contracts implemented here: `Sessions.enqueue`, `BeginTurn.Queued`, `QueuedInput`, `InputDelivery`, `TurnWriter.steers` and `take`; the core entry exports those schemas and `InputConflict`. `drain.take` defaults to all; reaction-only hosts select notices.
- `SessionChannelMemoryLive(options)` and `makeMemorySessionChannel(options)` (which also reports `retained`, the sessions it holds a hub for): core's `SessionChannel` for one process; `SessionChannelOptions`, `sessionChannelDefaults` (256 payloads per followed session).
- Config: `SessionsConfig`, `sessionsDefaults` (a 60 s lease, a keep-alive every 20 s, a turn time limit of 6 hours), `Ownership`; `HolderProcess`, where a process-owned turn's holder runs (what `ProcessLiveness.current` returns). The state the plugin keeps in the session head (`SessionsState`, `HeldTurn`, `InboxSlot`) is its own and stays inside the package.

The root names each export, and `quality/exports-have-consumers` checks each one: other packages' tests, the scripts and the docs import `sessionsPlugin`, `SessionsPluginLive`, `SessionsLive`, `SessionsConfig`, `sessionsDefaults` and `ProcessLiveness`, and the rest are tagged `@public` at their declarations (`sessionsPlugin`'s tag covers the default export).

- `src/memory-snapshot.adapter.test.ts`: atomic row/pointer publication with unchanged state, assigned offsets after CAS conflict, lifecycle and lease preservation, nested fork sequence overlap, future-media exclusion, selected corruption, invalid frontier, lost ACK identity and interrupted ACK waiters. - `src/waiting-input.adapter.test.ts`: honest storage failures, cached shutdown backlog, raw reads with invalid state, interrupted-ACK wakes for enqueue/create/fork/remove, the bounded set of refreshed sessions, and a turn's own writes signalling no waiting subscriber.
- `src/inbox-drain.adapter.test.ts` and `.entity.functions.test.ts`: discovery, concurrency, backoff, startup takeover (named, never this instance's own turn or one begun since), shutdown and cancellation; parking after the attempt limit or a deliberate stop, resumption only for a gone holder or an earlier process, a self-interrupting runner counted, and scans that read only changed sessions. - `src/sessions.adapter.test.ts`: one turn at a time, leases, activity, cancel and removal, check then append, contention and a broken log, the inbox, resuming, forks, admission, and the spans and log annotations, over the in-memory log.
- `src/opening-commit.adapter.test.ts`: a begin whose opening commit failed with a store error (stored all the same, or not), and a turn's time limit stopping with the turn.
- `src/turn-writer.entity.functions.test.ts`: the writer's transitions, as tables, phase by phase. `src/sessions-state.entity.functions.test.ts` distinguishes bounded notice attempts from retained queued input and cancellation acknowledgments.

- `src/session-channel.memory.adapter.test.ts`: core's `sessionChannelConformance` over the in-memory channel, its default and refused capacity, and a publish nobody follows keeping nothing. `packages/sdk/src/session-channel.journey.test.ts` streams a turn run by one request to a render feed another opened, through `shareTransient` and plugin-render's `sessionLive`.
