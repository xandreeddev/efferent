The kernel's graph can name things loosely: `withGraph({ hops, limit, names: "loose" })`, `near`'s `names`, and
`namedIn`'s fourth argument (`Naming`). A question's word names a thing's word without its accent marks, or one
letter away when both are five letters or more and hold no digit. `"exact"` stays the default.
